# aictrl.dev

> AI workflow orchestration platform for engineering teams that use AI coding agents.

aictrl.dev helps teams manage AI-assisted development through three pillars: Grounded Backlog (structured tasks with acceptance criteria and evidence-based approval gates), Skills Governance (standardized agent instructions treated as production code), and Knowledge Graph (contextual memory that improves agent accuracy and reduces token costs). The platform ensures AI agents prove their work rather than just promise it.
